Why privacy-first PDF processing matters

On supported local operations, processing is performed on your device rather than sending the original document to a PDF conversion server. Your device and browser still need to be protected, and sharing files is an explicit, separate action.

Core tools are free. Free individual signing adds a clickable JustPDFit.com watermark; removing it is an optional €1.99 offer. Remote two-person signing, PDF-to-Word, Word-to-PDF and PDF compression are under development.

Questions about JustPDFit

Is JustPDFit really free?

Yes. PDF creation from supported images and text, merging, splitting, JPG to PDF and simple text annotation are free without an account. Individual signing is free with a site watermark; removing that watermark may cost €1.99.

Are PDF files uploaded to a server?

For supported local browser tools, the source document stays on your device and is processed in your browser. If you choose to share a file, it is sent to the destination you select.

Does JustPDFit work on mobile?

Yes. The website works on modern mobile and desktop browsers; an iPhone and iPad app is also available.

Can I convert PDF to editable Word now?

Not yet. Editable PDF-to-Word, Word-to-PDF and advanced PDF compression tools are clearly marked as under development.
